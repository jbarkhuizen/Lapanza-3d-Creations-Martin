Custom 3D Printing in Centurion & Pretoria

Lapanza 3D Creative Lab is a family-run print workshop in Pierre van Ryneveld, Centurion. We design and print functional parts, replacement pieces, prototypes, toys and gifts to order — for customers around Centurion and Pretoria, and by courier across South Africa.

Bring us a 3D file if you have one, or just a photo, sketch or broken part if you don't — designing the model for you is half of what we do. Production typically takes 3-5 business days; collect from us in Pierre van Ryneveld, use Local Delivery around Centurion, or ship nationwide via PUDO Locker.

We print in every mainstream material — PLA for detail, PETG for working parts, ABS and ASA for heat and outdoor use, flexible TPU, and engineering-grade CPE. Not sure which fits your project? Our Materials Guide gives the honest short version, or just ask.
