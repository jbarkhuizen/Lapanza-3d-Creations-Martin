Silk PLA+

Order / enquire

SA Filament Silk PLA+ takes PLA to the next level. If you want breathtakingly beautiful shiny, silky prints, look no further. With higher heat deflection temperature, impact strength and improved durability, Silk PLA+ is the perfect PLA alternative for anyone who wants more “wow factor” from their prints. Silk PLA is normally known for poor layer adhesion and slow printing — SA Filament's formula prints at normal PLA temperatures and speeds.

Specifications

Print Temp190–230°C
Bed Temp50–65°C
Density1.25 g/cm³
Heat Distortion Temp61°C
Tensile Strength35 MPa
Elongation at Break25%
