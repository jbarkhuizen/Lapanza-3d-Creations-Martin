Quick Questions

How long does a printed item take?

Items are printed to order — allow 3-5 business days for production before dispatch or collection.

Can I choose the colour or material?

Usually, yes — use the item's Enquire link or the custom request box below and tell us what you'd like.

Can you print something that isn't listed?

That is half of what we do. Send a photo, sketch, file or part number through the custom design request and we will quote you.

Can I return a custom print?

Custom-printed items cannot be returned for change of mind, but faulty or misdescribed items are covered for 6 months — see our Returns Policy.
